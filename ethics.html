06 • RESPONSIBLE AI

Ethics & privacy.

Powerful technology needs responsible design. Privacy and uncertainty are part of this project.

🔐
PEOPLE FIRST

Respect the person behind the pixels.

Emotion recognition involves sensitive information. A thoughtful system seeks consent, collects as little data as possible and clearly explains its uncertainty.

01

Consent

People should know when the system is in use.

02

Minimize data

Collect only what is genuinely needed.

03

Be transparent

Explain that results are estimates.

04

Test fairly

Account for lighting, culture and datasets.

AI should assist, not assume. Never make a high-stakes decision using an emotion estimate alone.
