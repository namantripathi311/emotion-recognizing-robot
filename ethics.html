06 • RESPONSIBLE AI

Ethics & Privacy

Powerful technology needs responsible design. Privacy and uncertainty are part of the project.

🔐
PRIVACY & RESPONSIBLE AI

Respect the person behind the pixels.

Emotion recognition involves sensitive personal information. The system should obtain user consent, minimize data collection, avoid storing facial images unnecessarily, and clearly communicate that emotion predictions are uncertain.

01

Consent

People should know when a camera and emotion-estimation system is being used.

02

Data Minimization

Collect only what is needed and avoid unnecessary storage of faces or recordings.

03

Transparency

Explain that the result is an estimate rather than a direct measurement of feelings.

04

Fairness

Test carefully because lighting, culture, individuals and datasets can affect results.

WHY IT MATTERS

AI should assist, not assume.

Facial expressions can be ambiguous. A person may smile while feeling nervous, or look neutral while experiencing strong emotions. Responsible systems should avoid high-stakes decisions based only on an emotion prediction.
