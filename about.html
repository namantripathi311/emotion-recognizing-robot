What is the Emotion-Recognizing Robot?

An AI-based system that analyzes visible facial expressions and estimates a person's emotional state.

◡
FACIAL EXPRESSION
IN SIMPLE WORDS

A robot that can notice patterns

The Emotion-Recognizing Robot is an AI-based system that analyzes facial expressions using a camera and computer vision. It processes facial features and predicts the person's emotional state.

The important distinction is that the robot does not read minds. It makes an estimate based on observable signals, and that estimate can be wrong.
