What is the Emotion-Recognizing Robot?

An AI-based system that analyses visible facial expressions and estimates a person's emotional state — carefully, and with the uncertainty made visible.

Camera vision Pattern analysis Human-centred design
Facial expression Observable signal — not a feeling
In simple words

A robot that can notice patterns

EmotionBot is an AI-based concept that looks at facial expressions through a camera and computer vision. It locates the eyes, eyebrows and mouth, then compares those visible patterns with examples it has learned from.

The important distinction is that the robot does not read minds. It makes an estimate from observable signals — and that estimate can be wrong, especially across different people, cultures and lighting conditions.

Expression set

Six example categories

These are the labels the concept uses. Real emotion does not fit neatly into six boxes — which is exactly why the confidence score matters.

Happy

Raised cheeks, smiling mouth, relaxed brows.

Sad

Drooping eyelids, turned-down mouth corners.

Angry

Lowered brows, narrowed eyes, tense jaw.

Fear

Wide eyes, raised brows, tightened mouth.

Surprise

Raised brows, open mouth, wide eyes.

Neutral

Little visible tension, relaxed features.

An expression is behaviour, not truth. A smile can mean joy, politeness, nerves or habit. This project treats every estimate as a guess worth double-checking — never as a diagnosis.
