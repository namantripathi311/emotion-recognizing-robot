02 • PROCESS

How does it work?

Four simple stages connect computer vision, AI analysis and a possible robot response.

📷
STEP 01

Capture

The camera captures an image or video frame containing the person's face.

⌁
STEP 02

Detect

Computer vision locates the face and important facial features such as eyes, eyebrows and mouth.

🧠
STEP 03

Analyze

An AI model analyzes expression patterns and compares them with learned examples.

🤖
STEP 04

Respond

The robot identifies the most likely category and can choose a suitable response, movement, sound or display.

SYSTEM FLOW

From pixels to a robot response

Camera→Face Detection→Feature Analysis→Emotion Estimate→Robot
