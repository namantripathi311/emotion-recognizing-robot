Where could this be useful?

Emotion-aware interaction could help robots respond more naturally in many settings.

🎓

Educational Robots

Make learning robots more interactive and responsive.

👵

Elderly Assistance

Explore friendly interfaces that can notice visible signs of emotion.

🛎️

Customer Service

Help robots adapt their interaction style in public spaces.

🤝

Social Robots

Create more natural human-robot interaction experiments.

💬

Wellness Support Tools

Provide optional interaction cues, without treating estimates as diagnoses.

🏫

Smart Classrooms

Research how technology can adapt educational experiences.

🎮

Entertainment

Build games and robots that react to visible expressions.

🔬

Human-Robot Research

Study communication between people and intelligent machines.

Important: An emotion-recognition system should treat its output as an estimate, not a perfect measurement of someone's actual feelings.
