Can a robot read a face without assuming a mood?

This project explores how expression recognition works — and why visible cues are not the same as a person's inner state.

EmotionBot estimates visible patterns from a face, then responds carefully. It does not claim to know what someone is truly feeling.

The big idea

From a visible expression to a cautious response.

This project shows how a system can look at facial features, compare them to patterns, and still stop short of claiming certainty about a person's feelings.

The honest limit

Confidence is not certainty.

The model can estimate visible features on a face, but it cannot tell a person's mood, intent, or life story from a still image alone.

What the robot can estimate
  • Face position
  • Eyebrow and mouth shape
  • Visible expression pattern
Estimated confidence: 94%

What it cannot know
  • Inner feelings
  • Life context
  • Intent or cause
